1. Fibonacci Sequence & The Golden Ratio

Mathematics is the science of patterns in nature. The Fibonacci sequence generates spiraling structures found in pinecones, sunflowers, and seashells:

$$F_0 = 0, \quad F_1 = 1, \quad F_n = F_{n-1} + F_{n-2}$$ As $n \to \infty$, the ratio of consecutive terms approaches the Golden Ratio ($\phi$): $$\phi = \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{F_n}{F_{n-1}} = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2} \approx 1.6180339887...$$
Quick Check: Given the Fibonacci sequence $1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13$, what is the next term ($F_8$)?

🐍 Python Implementation (Fibonacci & Golden Ratio)

def fibonacci(n):
    seq = [1, 1]
    for i in range(2, n):
        seq.append(seq[-1] + seq[-2])
    return seq

terms = fibonacci(10)
golden_ratio_approx = terms[-1] / terms[-2]

print("Sequence:", terms)
print(f"Golden Ratio Approx: {golden_ratio_approx:.6f}")
