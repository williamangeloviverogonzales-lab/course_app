1. Industrial Motion & Kinematics

In industrial automation, predicting position ($d$), initial velocity ($v_0$), constant acceleration ($a$), and elapsed time ($t$) is fundamental for robotic arm movement and conveyor belt timing:

$$d = v_0 t + \frac{1}{2}a t^2 \quad \text{and} \quad v = v_0 + a t$$
An automated robot starting from rest ($v_0 = 0\text{ m/s}$) accelerates at $a = 2\text{ m/s}^2$ for $t = 4\text{ seconds}$. What total distance ($d$) does it cover?

🐍 Python Implementation (Kinematic Displacement)

v0 = 0.0  # initial velocity (m/s)
a = 2.0   # acceleration (m/s^2)
t = 4.0   # time duration (s)

# Distance formula d = v0*t + 0.5*a*t^2
d = (v0 * t) + (0.5 * a * (t ** 2))
v = v0 + (a * t)

print(f"Displacement (d): {d:.2f} meters")
print(f"Final Velocity (v): {v:.2f} m/s")
